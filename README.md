# SHIFT DERBY / 赛马 SHIFT

A bilingual pixel-art horse-racing game for **GitHub Game Off 2026: SHIFT**. Tap Shift to counter an attack or boost when there is no warning. Hold Shift to use Overdrive, and shift lanes to pass the pack. Meet the round funding gates and compete through eight rounds for the highest rider earnings.

## Play

- Desktop keyboard and mouse recommended. Browser builds open from the itch.io game page.
- Left or right Shift: tap to counter a warning. At other times, aim at a horse's head to brake or its tail to boost. Tap over empty track to boost yourself. Each move costs one orb. The stock increases from three to four in round 4 and to five in round 6, for all riders.
- Perfect counters charge one third of the Overdrive meter. Once full, hold Shift for 0.18 seconds to double running speed for five seconds. Click a nearby rival to lock on, then click the same rival again to ram. A successful attack knocks the rival down on contact.
- Horses run and choose lanes automatically. Use Shift and mouse buttons to manage boosts, counters and tactical interventions.
- The earnings standings appear after each race. Every rider is scored with the same round stake and their own displayed odds. These net scores add over the run and do not add or remove coins from the player's wallet. The eighth round determines the earnings champion.
- If the supported rider is the final earnings champion, the final standings appear for four seconds and then automatically play that rider's 7.5-second victory video and champion photograph.
- Mouse wheel: camera zoom. Tab: track overview. Hold and drag the minimap to look around.
- Click a horse and use Brake or Boost to intervene. Click the counter bar as an alternative to tapping Shift.
- Swap between English and Chinese in Settings.

## Run the source project

Open `project.godot` in Godot 4.7.2 and run the project. The supplied export presets expect the official Godot export templates for the same engine version.
